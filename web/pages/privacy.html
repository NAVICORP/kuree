Privacy

What Kuree keeps, why, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Who we are

Kuree is made by SkiFi. You can reach us at contact@skifi.co.

What Kuree keeps

  • Your sign-in. The account you sign in with, through the SkiFi login: Sign in with Apple (on iPhone), Google, or your email with a one-time code. From Apple or Google we get your name and email address (or the private relay address Apple makes for you) and an account ID; we never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Your name, if you give one, so other admins see who saved a change.
  • Your chits. What admins type in: the chit's name and amounts, the names of the people in it, their WhatsApp numbers if added, who has paid each month, late fines, who took the pot and how the draw went.
  • History. Earlier versions of each chit (up to 200), with who saved each one and when, so a mistake can be undone.
  • Notifications. If you turn on reminders, your phone's notification address (given by Apple or Google through Firebase Cloud Messaging) and the app's language, so Kuree can tell you when another admin marks payments, makes the draw or adds you as an admin. Collection day reminders are set on your phone itself. Turning reminders off, signing out or deleting your account removes the address.

What members see

Each chit has a private link that its admins share. Anyone with the link sees the chit's names, payments, fines and draws. Phone numbers never appear there. Admins can make a new link at any time, and the old one stops working straight away. These pages are hidden from search engines.

How it is used

Only to run Kuree: to sign you in, to show and save your chits, to send admins notifications about their chits, and to let admins send WhatsApp reminders from their own phone. Kuree itself does not message the people in a chit. We do not sell data, show ads, or use trackers or analytics.

Where it is kept

On SkiFi's own servers, with a copy made every night and kept for 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the app: Account, then Delete account. Your Kuree account and name are deleted; chits you own pass to their next admin; chits only you run are deleted with their history and links. Your SkiFi login is shared with other SkiFi apps, so it stays unless you tick Also delete my SkiFi login when you delete, or email contact@skifi.co from the address or number you sign in with and we will delete it too, or do the whole deletion for you. Nightly copies roll off within 30 days. See deleting an account.

People in a chit

If you are in a chit and want your name or number removed, ask its admin, or email us and we will pass it on.

Children

Kuree is meant for adults.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
