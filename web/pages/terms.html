The plain rules for using Kuree. Last updated 24 October 2026.

What Kuree is

Kuree keeps the record of a chit: who is in it, who has paid, late fines and who takes the pot each month. It does not hold, collect or move money. A chit is an arrangement between its members, and Kuree is not a party to it.

Admins

  • You are responsible for what you enter and for keeping it accurate.
  • Only add people's names and numbers when they are happy for you to.
  • Keep your chit's link to the group. Make a new one if it reaches the wrong people.
  • Check that running your chit follows the rules where you live.

The draw

The bottle draw picks one slot with the phone's secure random numbers before the animation starts, and every waiting slot has the same chance. The admin who runs it decides whether to save the result.

Fair use

Do not use Kuree to mislead people, to store anything unlawful, or to try to reach other people's chits. We may close accounts that do.

No guarantees

We work to keep Kuree running and your data safe, and every chit keeps its history. Kuree is still provided as it is, without guarantees, and SkiFi is not liable for losses arising from a chit or from using the app, as far as the law allows.
