Deleting an account

How to delete your Kuree account and what happens to your chits.

In the app

  • Open Kuree and tap your initials at the top.
  • Tap Delete account. To remove your SkiFi login as well, tick Also delete my SkiFi login. Then confirm.

By email

Email contact@skifi.co from the email you sign in with, or tell us the WhatsApp number you sign in with. We confirm it is you, then delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Kuree account, your name and your place as an admin.
  • Your SkiFi login (the WhatsApp number or email you sign in with) is shared with other SkiFi apps. Tick Also delete my SkiFi login in the app, or ask by email, and it is deleted too.
  • Chits only you run, with their history and members' links.
  • Chits you own with other admins are not deleted: they pass to the longest serving admin.
  • Nightly copies roll off within 30 days.
