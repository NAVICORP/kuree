Born in a real kuri.

Kuree started as the app behind the Boystown 5K Kuri in Blathur, Kerala. Now any group can run theirs on it.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Kuree hold any money?

No. Kuree only keeps the record. Members pay each other the way they always have.

Do members need to install anything?

No. They open the link you share, in any browser. Only admins use the app.

Who can make changes?

Only admins. The owner can add co-admins by WhatsApp number or email.

What can members see?

Names, who paid, fines, who took the pot and what comes next. Phone numbers stay with the admins.

Is the draw fair?

Every slot still waiting for the pot goes into the bottle once, and the phone's secure random numbers pick one. You can save the whole draw as a video for the group.

What if I mark something wrong?

Undo before you save, or bring back any earlier version from History.

When can I get it?
